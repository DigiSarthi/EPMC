Bas tum.
Hamesha tum.

Ruchi, tumse pyaar karne ki wajah sirf 10 nahi hain. Bas aaj ke liye itni hi likhi hain. Tumhari chhoti-chhoti baatein, hamari hasi, kabhi nok-jhok aur door rehkar bhi ek doosre ko yaad karna — ye sab mere liye bahut special hai. Main chahta hoon ki tum hamesha khush raho, apne sapne poore karo, aur kabhi ye mat bhoolna ki koi hai jo tumhe dil se chahta hai.

Waise toh tumse pyaar karne ki wajah bahut saari hain, lekin aaj sirf 10 batayi hain. Kyunki kutti, tu mere efforts ki value hi nahi karti! 😂❤️

Sirf tumhara, Gobind
