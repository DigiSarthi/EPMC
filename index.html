Pension application help, made simple.

Eastern Pension Mitra Services helps applicants understand their application status, find out why an application was rejected, correct documents and re-apply — with a real person to follow up with you.

Our servicesContact us

What we do

Support for applicants of the Madhu Babu Pension Yojana (MBPY) and similar pension schemes, from first check to final follow-up.

🔍

Application status check

We check where your application stands and explain what the status means in simple language.

❓

Rejection reason guidance

If your application was rejected, we help you understand the reason shown against it.

📄

Document help

We tell you which documents are needed and what to correct, using a clear checklist.

🔁

Re-application support

We guide you step by step so you can apply again with the right details and documents.

📞

Personal follow-up

An executive calls you, keeps track of your case and reminds you about the next step.

💳

Simple, clear payment

Service fee is paid by UPI to the name shown on the payment page. You always see who you are paying.

How it works

Four simple steps.

1

We contact you

Our executive calls you about your application.

2

We explain

You get to know the status and what needs correction.

3

Documents & re-apply

We guide you in collecting documents and applying again.

4

Follow-up

We keep following up until your case is completed.

Please note: EPMS Odisha is a private service provider. We are not a government office or website, and we are not connected with any government department. Approval of an application is decided only by the concerned government authority — we cannot guarantee approval. Always confirm the payee name shown on the payment page before paying.
