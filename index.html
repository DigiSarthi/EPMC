Our work

We support applicants of the Madhu Babu Pension Yojana (MBPY) and similar pension schemes — from the first check to the final follow-up.

🔍

Application status check

We look up where your application stands and explain what the status means, in simple language you understand.

❓

Rejection reason guidance

If your application was rejected, we help you understand the reason shown against it and what has to be fixed.

📄

Document guidance

We tell you exactly which documents are needed and which ones to correct, with a clear checklist.

🔁

Re-application support

We guide you step by step so that you can apply again with the right details and the right documents.

📞

Personal follow-up

One of our executives calls you, keeps track of your case and reminds you about every next step.

🤝

Help in your language

We talk to you in the language you are comfortable with, so nothing is confusing or left unclear.

Common questions

Short answers to what people ask us most.

Is EPMS Odisha a government office?

No. EPMS Odisha is a private service provider. We are not a government office and are not connected with any government department.

Will my application be approved if I take your help?

Approval is decided only by the concerned government authority. We cannot promise or guarantee approval. What we do is help you understand your case and apply correctly.

Which schemes do you help with?

Mainly the Madhu Babu Pension Yojana (MBPY) and similar pension schemes in Odisha. Contact us and we will tell you if we can help with your case.

How will you contact me?

Our executive will call you on the mobile number given in your application. You can also write to us on our email.

I want to work with you. What should I do?

Please visit our Career page and apply there.
